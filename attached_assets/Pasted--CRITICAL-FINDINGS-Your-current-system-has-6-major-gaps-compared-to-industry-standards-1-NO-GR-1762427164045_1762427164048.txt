⚠️ CRITICAL FINDINGS
Your current system has 6 major gaps compared to industry standards:
1. NO GRANDFATHERING ❌
When you change a plan's price from $99 to $149, all existing subscribers immediately see $149
Industry standard: Existing customers keep their original price (Stripe, Shopify do this automatically)
2. NO PLAN VERSIONING ❌
Plans are directly edited in the database - no history of what the plan looked like when users subscribed
Industry standard: Create new versions, keep old ones for reference and compliance
3. HARD DELETE RISK ❌
Plans can be deleted (protected only by database constraints)
No formal discontinuation workflow or user migration tools
Industry standard: Soft delete with migration paths to new plans
4. NO CHANGE NOTIFICATIONS ❌
Users aren't informed when plan prices or features change
Industry standard: 30-60 day advance notice via email/dashboard
5. NO AUDIT TRAIL ❌
Only tracks updatedAt timestamp - no record of who changed what or why
Industry standard: Complete audit log for compliance (SOC 2, GDPR)
6. NO MIGRATION WORKFLOW ❌
No tools to transition users from discontinued plans to new ones
Industry standard: Guided migration with incentives/discounts

✅ WHAT WORKS WELL
Your system has good foundations:
Comprehensive admin UI with 25+ plan attributes
Proper validation and security
isActive flag for activating/deactivating plans
Database constraints prevent accidental deletion
